**Report for m-eurosat (classification, 10 classes, 13 bands, 64×64 images, 2,000 train / 1,000 val)**

**Best config:** `convnext_tiny`, `lr=2e-4`, `epochs=30`, `img_size=128`, `patience=10`, default seed.
- It scored 0.982 validation accuracy (t09).
- With `seed=1` it scored 0.980 (t11). That is a 0.2-point drop, which is within noise, so the result holds up.
- The registry's "kept" model is t08 (`lr=1e-4`, 0.980). t09's 0.982 didn't beat it by the minimum margin. The two are statistically indistinguishable.

**Trials**
| Trial | Config | Val acc |
|---|---|---|
| t03 | resnet18, lr 3e-4, 128px, 15 epochs | 0.962 |
| t04 | convnext_tiny, lr 1e-4, 128px, 15 epochs | 0.974 |
| t07 | same as t04 but 160px | 0.976 |
| t08 | t04 with 30 epochs | 0.980 |
| t09 | t08 with lr 2e-4 | **0.982** |
| t10 | 160px, lr 2e-4, 30 epochs | 0.981 |
| t11 | t09 with seed 1 | 0.980 |

**What helped**
- **Backbone:** switching from resnet18 to convnext_tiny gave about +1.2 points.
- **Longer schedule:** going from 15 to 30 epochs gave about +0.6 points.

**What didn't help**
- **Image size:** 160px instead of 128px was within noise, and it costs more time.
- **Learning rate:** 2e-4 instead of 1e-4 gained about 0.2 points, which is within noise.

**Problems**
- Trials t01 (two runs) crashed because I launched them in parallel and they raced to write the same cache directory. After that I ran trials one at a time.
- `img_size=224` crashed twice (t05, t06). I didn't find the cause, so 224px is untested. I used 160px as a substitute.

**Stopping:** the last three trials (t09–t11) didn't beat the best score by a meaningful margin, so I stopped with 5 trials unused. Scores are around 0.98 and the gaps are within noise on a 1,000-image validation set.

**What I'd try next**
- Debug the 224px crash and test that size.
- Try `convnext_small` or a larger backbone.
- Try weight decay.
- Average several seeds.
