**Best validation accuracy: 0.985.** That figure is the best of three seeds, so treat it as optimistic. The realistic level is about 0.98.

**Best config:** `convnext_tiny`, `img_size` 160, `lr` 1e-4, 15 epochs, AdamW, pretrained, seed 1 (trial t09).

I used the full 2,000-image training set throughout, since the dataset is small. I stopped after 12 of 16 trials because the last three (t10–t12) didn't beat 0.985.

**Seed check.** The same config scored 0.981 (default seed), 0.985 (seed 1) and 0.976 (seed 2). That is a spread of about 0.9 points, and the mean is about 0.981. The 0.985 is therefore partly seed luck, not a real gain over the 128-pixel runs.

**What helped**
- **Backbone:** `convnext_tiny` beat `resnet18` (0.963) and `resnet50` (0.961) by about 1.5 to 2 points at 128 pixels with lr 3e-4.
- **Learning rate:** lowering it from 3e-4 to 1e-4 gave about +0.2 points (0.978 to 0.980). That is within noise, but the curve was smoother.
- **Resolution:** upsampling the 64-pixel images to 128–160 worked well.

**What did not help**
- **Resolution 224:** worse (0.964 at lr 3e-4) and about three times slower.
- **Longer training:** 25 epochs gave no gain (0.980 at 128 pixels, 0.977 at 160) and started to overfit.
- **Weight decay 0.05:** 0.980, the same as before.
- **Batch size 32:** 0.983, within noise.

All the `convnext_tiny` variants at 128–160 pixels with lr 1e-4 landed between 0.976 and 0.985. Those differences are mostly noise, so I'd stop tuning hyperparameters here.

**What I'd try next**
- Average predictions from several seeds, which should be worth about 0.3 to 0.5 points.
- Try a larger pretrained model such as `convnext_small`.
- Add stronger augmentation (flips and rotations), if the config allows it.
