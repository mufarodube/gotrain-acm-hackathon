**Best configuration (trial t04):** `convnext_tiny`, `img_size` 64, `lr` 5e-5, `weight_decay` 0.05, 15 epochs, full training set (19,992 images), seed 0. Its validation accuracy was **0.593**.

I stopped after 12 of the 16 trials. The last 8 trials in a row failed to beat 0.593, which is past the rule of stopping after 3.

**The seed check says the win is mostly noise.** Rerunning the best config with seed 1 gave 0.578, a drop of 1.5 points. Several other convnext_tiny configs (t03, t06, t07, t09, t11) landed between 0.58 and 0.59. So I'd call the real level about 0.58–0.59, and I wouldn't read the t04 score as better than those. The validation set has only 986 images, so one image is about 0.1 points.

**What helped**
- **Backbone:** `convnext_tiny` beat `resnet18` clearly (0.544 vs 0.426) in the first comparison, on a 2,000-image subset.
- **Full training data:** going from the 2,000-image subset to all 19,992 images lifted convnext_tiny from 0.544 to 0.588.
- **Lower learning rate:** 5e-5 with weight decay 0.05 gave a steadier validation curve than 2e-4, which peaked at epoch 3 and then fell. The final score was about the same.

**What did not help**
- **Other backbones:** `resnet50` (0.545), `resnet34` (0.534) and `efficientnet_b0` (0.519) were all worse. They also had noisy validation curves.
- **Bigger model:** `convnext_small` scored 0.584, no better than `convnext_tiny`.
- **Upsampling to 96 px:** 0.587, no gain, and it was slower.
- **Other changes:** weight decay 0.3 with batch size 128 scored 0.580. An intermediate lr of 1e-4 with 10 epochs scored 0.578.
- **Memory limit:** 128 px was refused because it needs about 12 GB of RAM against a 7 GB limit. 96 px was the largest size that fit.

**What I would try next**
- **Sentinel-2 bands only:** use just the 10 optical bands, or give the SAR and optical bands separate input handling. The SAR bands are noisy and may hurt.
- **Ensembling:** average the predictions from several seeds, which should smooth out the seed-to-seed variance.
- **Longer, gentler training:** a lower-lr run at 96 px with a longer schedule.

The gap between train and validation looks like a domain shift between the cities in the two splits, not a lack of model capacity. Bigger backbones and more regularisation did not help.
